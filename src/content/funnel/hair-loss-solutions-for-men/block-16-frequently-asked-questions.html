Frequently Asked Questions

These are the exact questions most men ask before choosing us. Get clear, honest answers to make a confident decision.

What happens in the consultation?

You'll speak 1-on-1 with our expert. We'll understand your current hair loss, show you suitable options, and guide you on what will look best — with full clarity and honesty.

Is it an in-person or online consultation?

You can choose either. We offer both in-person consultations at our studio and online video consultations — whichever is more convenient for you.

Will I get to see real samples or demos?

Yes. If you come in person, you can feel the hair systems and see demos. If it's online, we'll show detailed videos and client photos that match your hair condition.

Will I be pressured to buy during the consultation?

Absolutely not. Our consultation is about education and clarity — not pushing you to buy. You decide only if and when you're ready.

How long does the consultation take?

Usually 30 to 45 minutes. We take time to understand you and answer all your questions properly.

Will I know the total cost after the consultation?

Yes. We'll give you a clear breakdown of the cost depending on the type of system you choose — with no hidden charges.

Will it look fake?

No, even stylists can't tell.

Will I need to shave my head?

No, clip-on options available.

Will it damage my hair?

No, breathable and skin-safe.

Will people notice?

No, invisible even in selfies or close-ups.

Can I swim, shower and workout with my hair system on?

Absolutely, you can sleep, swim, shower, and exercise with the system on, we guarantee that the system will last through all weathers including hot and humid environments.

Are your hair systems itchy to the scalp?

Modern hair systems are made to be light and comfortable. The days of itchy systems have long passed.
