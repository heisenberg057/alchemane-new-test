From Consultation
to Final Look

Step 01 – Hairline Design & Consultation

We assess your face shape, age, and lifestyle before designing your ideal hairline.

Step 02 – SMP Session

Our specialists replicate real follicles using 3rl & 1rl micro-needles and carbon-based ink.

Step 03 – Build Gradually

2–3 sessions spaced 7–10 days apart, build density layer by layer for a natural look.

Step 04 – Final Look & Touch-Up

Instant confidence restored. Touch-ups are only needed every 18–30 months.
